Our workshop

Serious work by skilled hands

Topline Stainless Worx is a stainless steel fabrication and welding workshop based in Benoni, serving commercial and residential clients across Gauteng. We specialise in custom fabrication — built to spec, finished to a high standard.

From a single balustrade to a full commercial run, the standard is the same: clean welds, sharp edges, and a finish that holds up. No shortcuts.
