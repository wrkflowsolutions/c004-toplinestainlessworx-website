Who we are

Serious metalwork, done properly

Topline Stainless Worx is a professional stainless steel fabrication and welding workshop serving commercial and residential clients across Gauteng. We specialise in custom fabrication — built to spec, finished to a high standard.

From homeowners adding a balustrade or gate, to contractors needing reliable commercial supply, to industrial clients who need bespoke components made — we take on the work and deliver it clean, square, and on time.

We're a workshop, not a showroom. The value is in the welds you can't see flaws in, the edges that line up, and the finish that lasts. That's what we build our name on.
